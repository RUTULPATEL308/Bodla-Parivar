Delete My Account - Bodla Parivar

Bodla Parivar respects your right to control your personal data. This page explains how you can request deletion of your account and all associated data from our community app.

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your Bodla Parivar account and all associated data, please follow these steps:

Step 1: Send an email to yourname@gmail.com with the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - Bodla Parivar"
Step 2: Include the following information in your email:
  • Your full name as registered in the app
  • Your registered email address or phone number
  • Your request to delete your account and all associated data
  • Any additional details that help us identify your account
Step 3: Wait for our confirmation email. We will process your deletion request within 7 business days.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Account profile (name, email address, phone number)
  • Authentication credentials (password hash, login tokens)
  • Profile photo and personal details
  • Community notices you created
  • Offerings (ચઢાવો) you submitted or bid on
  • Complaints you filed
  • Push notification preferences and device tokens
  • All personal data associated with your account

What Data Will Be Kept (and Why)

Some data may be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons:

  • Anonymized analytics: Aggregated usage statistics (no personal identifiers) - retained indefinitely
  • Transaction records: Financial records related to offerings/bids - retained for 7 years as required by law
  • Legal compliance: Data required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce terms - retained as necessary
  • Security logs: Access logs for security and fraud prevention - retained for 90 days
⚠️ Important: Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered. All your data will be permanently removed and you will lose access to all community features. If you wish to use the app again, you will need to create a new account.
✅ Confirmation: You will receive an email confirmation once your account and data have been deleted.
