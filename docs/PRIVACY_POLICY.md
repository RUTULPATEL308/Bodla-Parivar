# Privacy Policy — Bodla Parivar

**Effective Date:** November 2026  
**App:** Bodla Parivar (com.bodla.parivar)  
**Developer:** Bodla Parivar Community

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Bodla Parivar ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application.

By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you register or log in, we collect:
- Email address
- Password (stored encrypted, never in plain text)
- Full name
- Profile photo (optional)

### Usage Data
We collect information about how you interact with the app:
- Notices you view or publish
- Offerings (ચઢાવો) you submit or bid on
- Complaints you submit
- Emergency contacts you access

### Device Information
We may collect:
- Device type and operating system
- App version
- FCM push notification token (for sending notifications)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Authenticate your account and keep you signed in
- Send push notifications about community notices, offerings, and emergencies
- Display community notices, offerings, and events
- Allow you to submit offerings, bids, complaints, and notices
- Improve and maintain the app

## 4. Data Storage

- Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device (Android SharedPreferences / iOS UserDefaults)
- Notice and offering data is cached locally for offline viewing (SQLDelight database)
- All data is transmitted over HTTPS to our Supabase backend

## 5. Data Sharing

We **do not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only used to operate the app.

## 6. Data Retention

- Account data is retained as long as your account is active
- You may request account deletion by contacting us
- Deleted notices and notifications are removed from the server within 30 seconds on all connected devices

## 7. Your Rights

You may:
- Access and update your profile information
- Delete your account and associated data
- Opt out of push notifications in device settings
- Request a copy of your data

## 8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures:
- HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
- Encrypted password storage
- Secure token-based authentication

## 9. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app.
